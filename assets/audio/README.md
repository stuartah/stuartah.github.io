# Ponderosa recordings

MP3 recordings supplied by Stuart Hadfield for the Music page: seven tracks from Live at the Fairview (13 February 2005) and three from the CDIS Sessions (2003). Fairview has 192 kbps listening copies made from the original 320 kbps MP3s. The smaller CDIS files retain their original bytes. Original uploaded archives are preserved separately. Track metadata is maintained in `data/music.json`, and source filenames and checksums are recorded in `docs/ponderosa-audio-provenance.json`.
