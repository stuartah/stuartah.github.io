## Search coverage and limits

Searched both web indexes for the exact album title, distinctive song titles, Neverlost Records, Stuart Hadfield, Vancouver, Burnaby, Fairview, CDIS and old Myspace variants. Checked regional-band and show archives, artist profiles, indexed concert histories, the recovered Myspace Dragon Hoard metadata (490,273 rows) and contemporary-publication leads.

No original Myspace URL, additional band-member identities or correctly identified band photographs were recovered. The recovered Myspace Ponderosa tracks concern a different Atlanta band. The later Vancouver band The Ponderosas and the Ponderosa festival are also distinct. No photos or biography from those projects are used.

Wayback services were unavailable on this run. UBC’s Discorder archive presented an access challenge, its documented API required registration, and other contemporary PDF leads returned missing or unavailable responses. These are retrieval limits, not evidence that historical material never existed. The site therefore keeps a concise biography grounded in the owner’s account, recordings and surviving release description, and links the historical gig listing without inventing further history.

## Rosa cover cleanup

The owner supplied a larger screenshot of the YouTube artwork on 5 November 2026 and requested a clearer cover. Neither web index located a higher-resolution original. YouTube has no maxresdefault for this video; its 640 × 480 sddefault contains only a small central artwork area. The supplied screenshot was therefore used for an image-generation cleanup that removes the outer black border and improves apparent clarity. The resulting 1024 × 1024 JPEG is an enhanced derivative, not a recovered master; indistinct fine details may be interpreted by the restoration. The original uploaded screenshot remains unchanged, and the former 480 × 360 thumbnail remains in the repository. See `rosa-image-provenance.json`. The page now displays the square image directly instead of enlarging a letterboxed thumbnail with CSS.
